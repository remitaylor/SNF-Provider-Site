Integrated Care Model

Comprehensive services under one umbrella.

Traditional models often bring separate vendors for primary care, mental health, diagnostics and oversight, each with its own systems and communication. IHP unifies them.

  • Comprehensive services under one umbrella
  • Coordination across clinical specialties
  • Continuity of care within post-acute settings

How We Partner

Working alongside your operators and clinical teams.

IHP becomes an extension of your team, with clear communication, shared goals and clinical support that fits your workflows.

01

Understand

We assess your facility’s needs, census, staffing, quality priorities and existing services.

02

Integrate

We align providers, schedules and communication with your administration and nursing teams.

03

Optimize

We monitor outcomes, meet regularly with leadership and refine services as your needs evolve.

Provider Communication

Consistent, responsive communication between IHP providers and facility staff.

Collaboration

Participation in care conferences, QAPI meetings and clinical huddles.

Clinical Integration

Shared care plans and coordinated follow-up across every IHP discipline.

Post-Acute Care Approach

Care designed for post-acute settings.

Our providers understand the clinical and regulatory realities of skilled nursing and other post-acute environments, and deliver care accordingly.

Post-Acute Service Delivery

On-site and coordinated clinical services built for post-acute residents.

Skilled Nursing & Beyond

Supporting skilled nursing facilities and other applicable care environments.

Ongoing Care & Coordination

Continuity from admission through discharge or long-term residency.
