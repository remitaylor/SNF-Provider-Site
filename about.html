Who We Are

A comprehensive clinical partner for post-acute care.

Integrated Health Partners (IHP) provides coordinated clinical services to skilled nursing facilities and post-acute care organizations. Rather than offering a narrow set of provider services, IHP brings primary care, medical directorship, psychological care, behavioral health, diagnostics and ancillary services together under one model.

Our purpose is simple: help our facility partners deliver better care to the residents they serve, while making clinical operations easier to manage.

Our Purpose

Strengthen care delivery in every facility we serve.

Powered by AMA

IHP is part of the AMA family of organizations, building on AMA’s operational experience and business-focused approach to healthcare.

Our Mission & Approach

Comprehensive care, coordinated with purpose.

We believe post-acute residents are best served when every clinical discipline works from the same plan, with the facility team as a true partner.

Commitment to Comprehensive Care

We cover the full range of medical, behavioral and diagnostic needs common in post-acute settings, so residents get what they need without fragmentation.

Integrated, Coordinated Services

Our providers share information and align on care plans across specialties, creating continuity from admission through discharge.

Quality & Facility Collaboration

We work alongside administrators, DONs and nursing staff to support quality programs, survey readiness and day-to-day clinical operations.
