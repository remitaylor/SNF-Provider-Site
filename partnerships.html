Why Partner With IHP

The benefits of one coordinated clinical partner.

IHP’s key differentiator is a comprehensive, integrated post-acute care model that brings multiple clinical and supporting services together, rather than a narrow set of provider services.

Comprehensive Clinical Offering

Primary care, medical directorship, psychological care, behavioral health, diagnostics and ancillary services from one organization.

Integrated Care Delivery

Every discipline works from shared plans, with consistent communication to your team.

A Single Coordinated Partner

One relationship, one point of accountability, and less vendor complexity.

Key Differentiators

Operational understanding from AMA, physician leadership, and a focus on measurable outcomes.
